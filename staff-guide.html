1.Signing in & two-factor authentication

Staff sign in at studiocenterauditions.com with their username and password, the same way clients do (see the client guide).

Turning on two-factor authentication (2FA)

Admins and producers can protect their accounts with a code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password, and so on).

  1. Open Settings from the account menu and follow Two-step sign-in (or go to Account security), then click Enable two-factor authentication.
  2. In your authenticator app, add an account and enter the secret shown under Add this secret to your authenticator app (or paste the otpauth:// link below it if your app accepts one).
  3. Type the 6-digit code from the app into Verification code and click Confirm and enable.

From then on, after your password you'll be asked for an Authentication code. Once verified, that browser won't ask again for 12 hours. To turn 2FA off, use Disable 2FA on the same page.

2.The dashboard & header

Your home page after signing in: what needs attention today.

Below the tiles: projects due soon, missing auditions, today's auditions by producer, recent activity, coordinator stats, the call list (each count opens that tab), and recent projects, each with a link to the full report or tool.

On a phone: the header folds into the ☰ menu (account options included), and the project page's takes and talent become one card per row.

4.Creating a project

A new project is one page: Basics, Description & specs, Clients, Files, and Talent. To start from a client's request, click Create project on it in New project requests (see section 15); the description, scripts, and matching client come with it.

What happens when you create it

  • Each cast talent gets the audition request by email, and their status becomes Emailed.
  • The team is notified of the new project.
  • With Start email to client: Send, the clients get the opening email with their sign-in details.

5.The project page

Everything about one project. Use the On this page links to jump between sections.

Extending the deadline

Working on a project together

Project pages update live. Above the project's title you'll see who else has it open, and what they're working on (for example Jane Casting · editing the due date or adding talent). When anyone saves a change (status, talent, takes, notes, files, clients, phases) it appears on everyone's screen within a second or so, with a short note such as Jane changed status · just now. You don't need to reload to see each other's work.

Clients' project pages update the same way: new and changed takes, status and notes appear for them as you save them. They don't see who on our team made a change.

“Studio Center Auditions has been updated.” When we release a new version of the site, pages that were already open show this message with a Reload button. Finish what you're typing, then click Reload. If something you just did failed, do it again after reloading.

6.Notes

Internal notes for the team. Status changes, emails sent, and deadline changes are added automatically, so the notes double as the project's history.

Notes from clients appear separately under Client notes.

7.Scripts & reference files

Click the Scripts or Reference files heading on the project page to open it, then drop files in (or click to browse). Scripts are PDFs; reference files can be anything. Each file can be downloaded or deleted from its row.

8.Posting auditions

When a take is assigned to a talent, their status on the project changes to Posted automatically. Tick takes to publish, unpublish, assign talent to, or delete several at once. Each row also shows plays, who posted it and when, and the clients' average rating.

Clients see new takes right away. A published take shows up in their portal immediately and counts toward their “new takes” badge.

Take numbers

Published takes are numbered 1, 2, 3 from the top of the list, so you and the client can talk about “#4”. New uploads go to the bottom with the next number. Unpublished takes show a dash; they get a number when you publish them. Because the numbers follow the order, dragging takes or publishing an earlier one renumbers the ones after it, for the client too.

New versions of a take

When a client asks for another read, add it to the same take instead of posting a separate one: choose Upload new version from the take's ⋯ menu, or upload a file with exactly the same name as the take (from the upload box or the watch folder). The take keeps its name, number, ratings, shortlist and comments, and the earlier version is kept.

Clients see the take as v2 · Updated and can play every version too. Comments are marked with the version they were about (“on v1”), and their marks on the player's waveform show only on that version. The new version is checked for audio problems like any upload. Deleting a take deletes all its versions.

10.Clients & client emails

Main clients review the project in the portal and get the project emails. Additional clients can see the project and its takes but get no project emails.

Each email asks for confirmation before sending and is recorded in the activity log; the buttons show how many times each has gone out. Clients who've turned on Do not email me are skipped, and you're told who.

11.Phases

Track the project's progress through Casting, Talent Notification, and Posting and Publishing. Set each phase's status from its menu; the progress bar in the header and on the projects list follows along.

12.Review links & activity

Review links on the project page work like the clients' Share for review (see the client guide): a view-only link to the published takes that expires after the days you choose. Use it for an agency or a client's colleague who has no account; Turn off stops a link working. The Activity log at the bottom lists who did what on the project.

13.Talent roster & typecasts

Talent in the header lists every voice on the roster.

Open a talent to see their details, projects (with each project's status and due date), and contact log; Edit uses the same form as Create talent.

Typecasts are the tags used to describe voices (e.g. Warm, Conversational, British). Each one shows how many talent have it. Rename it and the talent are updated too; drag to change the order; add several at once by typing them separated by commas.

14.Users

Users in the header lists every account, with All, Clients, and Staff tabs and one search over names, emails, companies, usernames, and phones. Click a name to open the user's profile, which lists a client's projects.

17.Your settings

Settings in the account menu holds your profile and email signature, Do not email me (no project or notification emails; password resets still arrive), Two-step sign-in (see section 1), Deadlines in your calendar (a private calendar link of project due dates for Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar), and API keys for the watch-folder uploader.
