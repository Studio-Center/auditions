1.Signing in

Studio Center creates your account and emails you your sign-in details when your first project starts.

  1. Go to studiocenterauditions.com.
  2. Enter your username and password, then click Sign in.
  3. If you see a “Verify you are human” box, tick it. Most of the time the check happens invisibly.

Forgot your password

  1. Click Forgot your password? on the sign-in page.
  2. Enter your username and click Send reset link.
  3. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.
No email? Check your spam folder. For your privacy, the page shows the same message whether or not the username exists, so double-check the spelling too.

2.Your projects

After you sign in, you'll see the projects you're part of.

Click a project to open it.

3.Reviewing takes

Each project page lists every audition (take) we've posted for you. New takes appear throughout the day until the deadline.

Click the Name or Rating heading to sort by it; click again to reverse.

No takes yet? You'll see “Auditions are on the way.” We post takes as talent record them; check back later in the day.

4.Comments on a take

Tell us what you think of a particular read: “love the warmth”, “can she try it slower?”. Our team sees each comment and can reply right under it.

Comments pinned to a moment show as marks on the player's waveform. Click 1 comment (or Comment) again to close the thread.

5.Listening

Click Play on any take. A player opens along the bottom of the page and stays there while you scroll, so you can keep rating and shortlisting as you listen.

Starting another take stops the one that's playing, so you never hear two at once. With Play next automatically on, the player moves down the list by itself; press N or P to skip ahead or back.

6.Booking talent

  1. Tick Shortlist on the takes you want to book (favorites are added automatically). The Shortlisted view shows just those.
  2. In the bar at the bottom of the page, click Book N shortlisted.
  3. In Book Auditions, check the takes in the list (you can play them again here), then click Book.

Booked takes are marked Booked, the project shows Auditions complete, and our team is notified right away. You'll get an email confirming your selections, and we'll contact you about scheduling.

Booking is final from your side. If you booked the wrong take, reply to the confirmation email or contact your Studio Center producer and we'll sort it out.

7.Downloading files

To download one take, click its file name (or use ⋯ › Download this take). To download several at once as a zip file, click Download in the bar at the bottom of the page:

The number next to each choice is how many takes it includes; choices with nothing in them are greyed out. Large zips can take a moment to prepare before the download starts.

8.Sharing takes

Below the takes, you can send them somewhere else, or let colleagues without an account listen.

People with a review link can play the takes, with the same player, but can't rate, comment, book, or download. Links you've made are listed in the same panel; Turn off stops one working.

9.Notes for our team

Use Notes for Studio Center at the bottom of the project page for feedback about the whole project. For feedback on one take, comment on it instead (section 4).

10.On your phone

Everything works on a phone. Each take is a card with the full file name and the same controls as on a computer:

The top bar folds into the ☰ menu, which also holds your account options (section 13).

11.Keyboard shortcuts

On a computer, you can review a project without the mouse. The highlighted row is the one the keys act on. A short reminder sits just above the takes list.

KeyDoes
↑ ↓Move between takes
SpaceAdd the take to your shortlist, or take it off
FFavorite or unfavorite
1 to 5Rate the take; 0 clears your rating
N / PPlay the next / previous take

Shortcuts pause while you're typing in a comment or note.

12.Requesting a new project

Anyone can ask us to cast a new project at studiocenterauditions.com/new-project; no account needed. If you're signed in, click Start a New Project (or New project in the top bar) and your details are already filled in.

Your request goes straight to our team, and a producer will be in touch to set up casting. If a “Verify you are human” box appears, tick it before sending.

13.Your account

Click the person icon at the right of the top bar to open the account menu. On a phone, tap ☰; the same options are listed there.

Settings opens Your profile:

  • Your details: name, company, and phone; keep these current so we can reach you.
  • Signing in: your email and username; Email me a password reset link sends a link to set a new password.
  • Do not email me: stops project and notification emails from us. Password reset links you ask for still arrive.
  • Appearance: light, dark, or match your device.
  • Deadlines in your calendar: a private link that adds your projects' due dates to Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar. Make a new link turns the old one off.

14.Troubleshooting

Sign-in says to wait

After several sign-in attempts in a row, sign-in pauses for a minute to protect your account. Wait a minute, then try again, or reset your password.

A project is missing

You only see projects you've been added to. If a colleague can see a project you can't, ask your Studio Center producer to add you. Closed projects are under Finished.

A take won't play

Check your device's volume and that the browser tab isn't muted, then try again. If one particular take never plays, comment on it and we'll take a look.

Still stuck?

Contact your Studio Center producer, or visit studiocenter.com. You can get back to this guide any time from Help guide in the account menu or at the bottom of every page.
