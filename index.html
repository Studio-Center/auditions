Quick start for clients

  1. Sign in at studiocenterauditions.com with the details from your welcome email.
  2. Open a project. Projects with new auditions show a “new takes” count.
  3. Play takes, rate them with stars, and shortlist the ones you like (favorites are shortlisted too).
  4. Click Book in the bar at the bottom of the page. We'll take it from there.

Common questions

I forgot my password.

Click “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in page and enter your username. We'll email you a link to set a new one. More

Where are my new auditions?

Projects with takes added since your last visit show a “new takes” count, and new takes are marked “New” inside the project. More

How do I download the takes?

Click a file name for one take, or click Download in the bar at the bottom for a zip of all, shortlisted, or booked takes. More

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Every page works on a phone: each take is a card with the same controls, and a bar at the bottom handles booking and downloads. More

Where do I sign out or change my settings?

Click the person icon at the right of the top bar (on a phone, tap ☰). It has the theme switch, this guide, your settings, and Sign out. More

I can't see a project my colleague can.

You only see projects you've been added to; closed ones are under Finished. Ask your Studio Center producer to add you.

How do I start a new project?

Fill in the form at studiocenterauditions.com/new-project; no account needed. A producer will be in touch to set up casting. More

Can a colleague without an account listen?

Yes: create a review link under Share for review on the project page. They can listen, but not rate, comment, or book. More
